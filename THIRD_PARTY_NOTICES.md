## KaTeX

This extension bundles KaTeX 0.16.11 for math rendering.

- Project: https://katex.org/
- License: MIT
- The bundled license text is in `media/katex/LICENSE`.

KaTeX is used unmodified. The extension loads its CSS and JavaScript from
`media/katex/` in both the webview and the optional browser UI.
